## 🔭 Research Overview
I study planetary atmospheres and interiors by combining physical models with Bayesian inference and machine learning to infer what cannot be observed directly.

At **CEA**, I study the dynamics of Earth's atmosphere between 15 and 130 km, a region that weather and climate models still constrain poorly. I use infrasound generated by ocean swell (microbaroms), recorded by the global infrasound network of the Comprehensive Nuclear-Test-Ban Treaty, to measure how winds and temperatures vary from days to years.

During my PhD and first postdoc at the **Observatoire de Paris**, I developed self-consistent atmosphere–interior models of giant planets and sub-Neptunes, and machine-learning surrogates to break degeneracies in planetary parameters. I also contributed to the discoveries of TWA 7 b and HD 143811 b.

## 💻 Open Source Software
* **[fuzzycore](https://github.com/ChristianSWilkinson/fuzzycore):** Python framework for the interior structure of giant planets and sub-Neptunes, including dilute (fuzzy) cores.
* **[exoweave (HADES)](https://github.com/ChristianSWilkinson/exoweave):** Couples the ExoREM radiative–convective atmosphere model to fuzzycore to produce self-consistent planetary profiles from the top of the atmosphere to the core.
